About Me

My main interest is artificial intelligence and machine learning, especially understanding how models work and finding ways to improve them. I've fine-tuned language models using different techniques, experimented with architectural changes, and built my own AI servers to run models locally.

I'm interested in more than just using existing AI tools. I want to understand the systems behind them, from training and inference to the hardware and software needed to run them efficiently. I also enjoy turning experiments into working applications.

Outside of programming, I've spent over four years in Scouting, completed a 146-mile trek at Philmont Scout Ranch, and planned and led a 150-hour Eagle Scout service project for the City of Mountlake Terrace. I also play competitive hockey and previously played violin in my school's orchestra.
